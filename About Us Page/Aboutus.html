ABOUT US

Over 2 Decades of Excellence in Military Headgear Manufacturing,
Serving the Philippines with Pride and Dedication

Jimmy's Military Caps & General Merchandise began in 1990 as a small single-proprietorship business. By 1996, the company expanded into hat-making and began producing Pershing caps, WAC caps, shako, and other types of military headgear. What started as a modest venture grew steadily as the company earned the trust of uniformed institutions through consistent quality and dependable service.

Over the years, Jimmy's became a long-term supplier for several major academies. Since 2000, we have been providing shako, dress caps, and complete paraphernalia for the Philippine National Police Academy (PNPA), followed by the Philippine Military Academy (PMA) in 2001. In 2003, we also secured supply partnerships with the Philippine Merchant Marine Academy (PMMA) and the Maritime Academy of Asia and the Pacific (MAAP). The company later achieved key industry recognitions, including PhilGEPS registration in 2008 and PNP certification in 2009 for meeting official standards for Pershing caps.

Our work continued to expand as we became the supplier of pilot headgear for Philippine Airlines and PAL Express starting in 2010, and an accredited provider of Pershing caps for the Bureau of Fire Protection in 2016. For more than 25 years, Jimmy's Military Caps & General Merchandise has remained committed to producing durable, well-crafted headgear at fair and reasonable prices, upholding the same promise that has guided the business since the beginning.
